Why does my shift show 7 or 9 hours when I worked 10pm to 6am?

On the nights the UK clocks change, that shift really is an hour shorter or longer. ShiftPilot pays the time actually worked.

Is the figure what I will be paid?

It is an estimate of gross pay, before tax and National Insurance, from the rates you enter. Your payslip is the final word.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings > Back up my data, save the file somewhere you can reach, then on the new phone choose Settings > Restore from a backup. Restoring replaces what is on that phone.

How do I cancel ShiftPilot Pro?

iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > ShiftPilot. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What happens if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your shifts and jobs stay, and pay is shown at your basic rate until you subscribe again.
