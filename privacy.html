ShiftPilot privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Provided by WIGL PROJECT, contact webdevelopment.tech1@outlook.com.

What stays on your phone

Your jobs, pay rates, shifts, notes and settings are stored in a database on your device. ShiftPilot has no account and no server of its own, and it does not send this information to us.

What is shared, and only when you choose

If you create a backup or a CSV export, the iOS share sheet sends that file wherever you pick. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives purchase information and a random app user ID, not your shifts or pay. See revenuecat.com/privacy. We do not receive your payment details.

Advertising and tracking

ShiftPilot shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. Settings > Erase all my data deletes it, and so does deleting the app, unless you have kept a backup elsewhere.

Children

ShiftPilot is meant for people in work and is not directed at children.

Your rights and changes

You can contact us at the address above about your data, and you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We will update this page if anything changes.
